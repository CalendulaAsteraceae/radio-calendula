About Me

Hi, I’m Měryem! My full Society name is Měryem al Ixbilǐa, also spelled Méryem al Ixbilía, Meryem al-Ishbiliyya, مَرْيَم اَلِشْبِيلِيَّة, and any number of other variations. My persona is an Andalusian woman from Almoravid Seville. In the SCA, I do ridiculous amounts of heraldry, and less ridiculous amounts of calligraphy, webministry, and other pursuits.

I live in the shire of Caer Gwyn in the Middle Kingdom.
