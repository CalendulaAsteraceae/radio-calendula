About Me

Hi, I’m Měryem! My full Society name is Měryem al Ixbilǐa, also spelled Méryem al Ixbilía, Meryem al-Ishbiliyya, مَرْيَم اَلِشْبِيلِيَّة, and any number of other variations. My persona is an Andalusian woman from Almoravid Seville. In the SCA, I do ridiculous amounts of heraldry, and less ridiculous amounts of calligraphy, webministry, and other pursuits.

I live in the shire of Caer Gwyn in the Middle Kingdom, and can be contacted at meryemsca@gmail.com.

  • Local Offices:
    • Caer Gwyn Pursuivant, November 2025–present
    • Caer Gwyn Deputy Webminister, July 2026–present
  • Kingdom Offices:
    • Opinicus Herald (notifications deputy), April 2026–present
  • Society Roles:
    • Commenting member of the College of Arms, December 2025–present

For anyone who’s trying to sneakily learn my scroll preferences, look no further!

  • My favorite things:
    • Wording based on period texts
    • Calligraphy and illumination based on period exemplars
    • Anything the scribe is excited about, ideally with an infodump afterwards
  • Other suggestions:
    • Scrolls suited to my persona
    • Scrolls suited to the scribe's persona
    • Scrolls suited to the persona of the monarch or other noble giving the award
