Augmentation Statistics: Or, Most Augmentations Aren’t Registered

By Měryem al Ixbilǐa
meryemsca@gmail.com

Published 18 September 2026

In May of 2026, Kallinikos Gavras asked how many of the augmentations of arms awarded by the Middle Kingdom had been registered. I am easily distracted by spreadsheets and spent the next five months, on and off, compiling statistics on augmentations awarded and registered across the Known World.

The data used in this post comes from each kingdom’s order of precedence and the SCA Ordinary & Armorial. I put the data into a spreadsheet, and did my best to match up names in the OPs with registered names, accounting for recipients whose awards appear in multiple kingdoms’ OPs (sometimes under different names) and cases where the same name in different OPs refers to different people. In a few cases, I might have assigned an augmentation to the wrong kingdom, and it's possible that I’m missing some augmentations, but I believe the data is mostly correct and complete.

The data and computations are available in a Google Sheets spreadsheet.

The statistics for augmentations awarded are based on awards given before 1 September 2026. The O&A contains items registered through July 2026, so statistics for augmentations registered are based on awards given before 1 May 2026, as anything later could not have completed the registration process.
