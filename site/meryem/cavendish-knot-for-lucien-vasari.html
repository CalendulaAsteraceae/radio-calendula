Cavendish Knot for Lucien Vasari

By Měryem al Ixbilǐa
meryemsca@gmail.com

Published 18 November 2026

I had the honor of doing, as my first award scroll, the wordsmithing and calligraphy for my friend Lucien Vasari’s Cavendish Knot (the Middle Kingdom’s award-level order for rapier combat). The illumination was done by Mikhail of Lubelska for the Scroll Blank Derby at Fall Coronation.

I based the hand for the scroll on Arte de la lengua mexicana (1547), an early grammar of Nahuatl by Andrés de Olmos. If I’d had more time, I would have learned a hand which better matched the text or the recipient’s persona (and perhaps contained the letter k), but I decided it was more important to use a hand I could execute well so I could make a beautiful scroll for my friend. It is at least a hand that makes sense coming from me with my various Iberian personæ, and course we can call it a creative anachronism.

The text was based on Letters Patent to Sir Humfrey Gylberte, June 11, 1578 and a Charter to Sir Walter Raleigh, 1584 from Elizabeth I, with some words of praise adapted from the fencing manual Vincentio Saviolo his Practise (1595), since Lucien studies Saviolo’s method.

I noticed after completing the scroll that I had duplicated a word. It was too late to fix, and I have not yet learned how period scribes corrected errors, so elected to leave the error in. Titivillus won’t starve anytime soon!

Scroll Text

As Written (Less Errors)

Radagaſius by right of armes King of the Middle, and Ceinwen our Queen. To all people to whom theſe preſents ſhall come, greeting.

¶ Knowe yee that of our eſpecial grace, certaine ſcience, and meere motion, we haue created, and by theſe preſents we create our truſtie and welbeloued ſeruant Lucien Vaſari, Armiger: whoſe valour inforceth all ſoldiers to acknowledge them an Achilles: whoſe benigne protection and prouiſion for ſtrangers, maketh them reported off as theyr ſafe ſanctuary: a companion of our Order of the Cauendiſh Knot.

¶ And we do likewiſe by theſe preſents giue licenſe to the ſaid Lucien Vaſari, that they ſhall and may at all and euery time, and times hereafter, bear the signe of ſayd Order, four Cauendiſh knots conioined in croſſe vert, and enioye to them the right, franchiſes, and dignities of this rank.

¶ In witneſſe whereof, &c. Witneſſe our ſelues in Shattered Cryſtal the 3. day of October. Anno Soc 61.

Gloss

Radagasius by right of arms King of the Middle, and Ceinwen our Queen. To all people to whom these presents shall come, greeting.

Know ye that of our especial grace, certain science, and mere motion, we have created, and by these presents we create our trusty and well-beloved servant Lucien Vasari, Armiger; whose valor inforceth all soldiers to acknowledge them an Achilles; whose benign protection and provision for strangers, maketh them reported of as their safe sanctuary; a companion of our Order of the Cavendish Knot.

And we do likewise by these presents give license to the said Lucien Vasari, that they shall and may at all and every time, and times hereafter, bear the sign of said Order, four Cavendish knots conjoined in cross vert, and enjoy to them the right, franchises, and dignities of this rank.

In witness whereof, etcetera, witness our selves in Shattered Crystal the third day of October, Anno Societatis 61.
